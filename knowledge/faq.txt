Customer Support FAQ

Password Reset:
Customers can reset their password from the login page by selecting the Forgot Password option and following the instructions sent to their registered email.

Account Login:
If a customer cannot log in, they should verify their email and password and try the password reset option if necessary.

Billing:
Customers can contact support for questions about charges, duplicate charges, refunds, invoices, and payments.

Technical Issues:
Customers experiencing application errors, crashes, or other technical problems should provide details about the issue and any error messages.

Pricing and Subscription Plans:
Sawantflix offers subscription plans for accessing premium streaming content.

Basic Plan:
The Basic plan costs ₹19 per month.
The Basic plan provides access to the available Sawantflix streaming content.

Customers can contact support for information about available plans, pricing, subscription status, payments, and product features.
